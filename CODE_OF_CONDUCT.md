# Code of Conduct

KamKontroller aims to be a welcoming and constructive project for everyone, regardless of
experience, identity, background, or viewpoint. Everyone participating in project spaces is
expected to help maintain that environment.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions, reviews, and issue reports.
- Critique ideas and code on their merits; do not attack or demean people.
- Share disagreement constructively and remain open to other perspectives.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information, private footage, or
  identifying device logs without permission.
- Accept reasonable correction when your conduct or contribution has negatively affected someone.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and
unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable. Publishing private information or imagery without
consent is also unacceptable. This applies in project discussions, reviews, and other spaces where
someone is representing or participating in KamKontroller.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report a concern privately to the project maintainers using the repository host's private contact
or reporting channel. Include relevant context and links when safe to do so. Do not post reports
containing private details publicly.

Maintainers will review reports, protect the privacy of people involved as far as practical, and
decide on an appropriate response. Responses may include a warning, removal of harmful content,
temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces. Maintainers may act on behavior that
they reasonably believe violates this code.

## Scope

This code applies in project-maintained spaces and whenever someone is acting on behalf of the
project. It also applies to conduct outside those spaces when it is directly connected to project
participation and has a serious effect on the safety or inclusion of contributors.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is written for KamKontroller and informed by common open-source community
standards.
